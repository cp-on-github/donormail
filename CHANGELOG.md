# Changelog

Changes users would notice, newest first. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-26

First release.

- Reads contacts and gifts from CSV or Excel files
- Reads QuickBooks Online reports
- Emails each donor a PDF letter
- Makes PDFs to print and mail for donors without email
- Handles cash, goods received, non-cash, and third-party gifts
- `check` finds problems before anything is written
- `send` delivers the emails after you review them
- Letter and email wording can be changed
